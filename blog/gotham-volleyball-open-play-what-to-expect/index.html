Surviving Gotham Volleyball Open Play: What to Expect

May 1, 2024 • By Author •

My first time signing up for an open play at PowerPlay, I almost didn't go. I was convinced everyone there was going to be an intense, hyper-competitive club player and I was going to ruin their games by shank passing every ball directly into the net. If you are feeling that exact specific anxiety, take a deep breath.

The Vibe

Gotham open plays are divided by skill level. If you sign up for the beginner or low-intermediate sessions, people expect you to make mistakes. You will see people serve into the net. You will see people run into each other. It is fine. The environment is incredibly supportive, and there's almost always a GLOD (Gym Leader on Duty) keeping the energy positive and the courts organized.

How it Works

When you walk into the gym (usually somewhere in Manhattan or Brooklyn, like PowerPlay), you check in with the GLOD. They'll tell you which court you are assigned to. From there, it's just pickup volleyball. Teams are usually shuffled around so you get to play with different people, which makes it a great way to meet players if you are new to the city.

Unspoken Rules

  • Call the ball: Even if you don't get there, yelling "Mine!" prevents collisions.
  • Don't steal the setter's ball: If it's the second touch and you aren't the setter, let the setter take it (unless they yell for help).
  • Roll the ball under the net: This sounds obvious, but people get annoyed when you toss the ball over the net and hit someone in the head.

The best thing you can do at your first open play is just introduce yourself. The Gotham community is massive but very friendly, especially to newcomers. Show up, laugh at your bad touches, and try to get better at the good ones.
