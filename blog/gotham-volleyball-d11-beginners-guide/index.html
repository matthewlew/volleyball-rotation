The Absolute Beginner's Guide to Gotham Volleyball D11

May 1, 2024 • By Author •

When I first looked into joining Gotham Volleyball, the sheer scale of it was intimidating. It's the largest LGBTQ+ recreational volleyball league in the country. There are thousands of players, dozens of divisions, and a whole dictionary of acronyms you're expected to just know. If you are an absolute beginner looking at the beginner division—D11—this guide is for you.

What is D11?

Gotham D11 is specifically for beginners. It's designed for people who have never played organized volleyball before, or people who haven't touched a volleyball since middle school gym class. You are not expected to know how to run a 5-1 rotation or jump serve. The goal is to learn the basics, get some touches, and most importantly, not be a jerk about it.

My first season, I rotated the wrong direction, stood in the wrong spot, and absolutely whiffed more serves than I care to admit. Nobody yelled at me. In D11, the volleyball is honestly secondary. The community is the real subject. We're all just trying to get better while making some friends along the way.

How to Join

The hardest part of Gotham is actually getting in. Because of gym space in NYC, demand far exceeds supply. You have to go to tryouts, which sounds terrifying but is actually more of an evaluation to make sure you are placed in the correct division for your skill level. If you are a true beginner, D11 is where you want to aim.

Once you are drafted onto a team (shoutout to my team, the Rebels, and our incredibly patient captain, Peter), you play matches every week. There are also practices, which is where the real improvement happens. The GLOD (Gym Leader on Duty) will be there to make sure everything runs smoothly.

The Bottom Line

Don't be afraid to show up, be terrible, and learn. The community is incredibly welcoming. Just remember to call the ball, try to bend your knees, and bring a good attitude.
