Making Friends in NYC Adult Sports Leagues

May 1, 2024 • By Author •

New York City is famously a difficult place to make friends as an adult. Everyone is busy, scheduling a simple coffee requires an Outlook invite three weeks in advance, and the sheer volume of people can make the city feel incredibly isolating. When I moved here, I struggled to find a consistent community.

The Power of Forced Proximity

This is where adult sports leagues come in. The secret to making friends isn't actually having exactly the same interests—it's just seeing the same people consistently over time. In sociology, they call it "propinquity." I just call it Gotham Volleyball.

When I got drafted to the Rebels in D11, I didn't know anyone on my team. Our captain, Peter, somehow managed to corral eight strangers who had never played together into a functioning unit. We had to communicate, we had to rely on each other, and we had to fail together. Nothing bonds a group of people faster than losing the first set 25-12 and having to laugh it off.

From the Court to the Bar

The actual sport is really just an excuse for the post-game hangout. Every week after our matches, we head to a bar. You start by talking about the game—who had a great hit, who absolutely whiffed a serve—and eventually, over the course of a 10-week season, you start talking about your actual lives.

If you are struggling to make friends in NYC, I cannot recommend adult sports leagues enough. Even if you are terrible at the sport (and believe me, I was terrible when I started), the shared experience of just trying is enough to build a community.
