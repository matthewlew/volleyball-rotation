About the Author

Hi, I play in D11 (a beginner division) at Gotham Volleyball NYC, the largest LGBTQ+ recreational volleyball league in the country. I'm a player on the Rebels, captained by Peter, and I built this free, no-ads, no-login 4-2 volleyball rotation app because I kept seeing teams struggle with lineups.

I started writing a blog to document my experience in the league and share what I've learned, from surviving open play to making friends in adult sports leagues. If you found this app useful, feel free to share it with your team.
